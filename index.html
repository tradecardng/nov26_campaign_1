FAQ

Questions

Do I need to sign up?
No. Every completed trade from 14 Nov to 6 Dec counts automatically.
Which trades count?
Completed (settled) gift card trades, based on your naira payout. Rejected, cancelled or reversed trades don't count.
Do I get every tier's reward?
Each tier shows your total reward. If you reach Tier 3, you receive ₦21,000 in coupons in total, credited as you reach each tier.
When do I get my coupons?
Within 48 hours of reaching a tier. They're valid for 14 days and appear on your Coupons page.
My coupon isn't working.
Update TradeCard to the latest version, then check that your trade's payout meets the coupon's minimum. Still stuck? Chat with support in the app, or contact us at service@tradecard.store.
Does the weekly draw still run?
Yes. The same trades earn draw tickets (₦100,000 = 1 ticket) and count toward your tier.
